# Documentation Ownership

Keep generic rules, project facts, and client-specific instructions separate.
Portable policy belongs in reusable instruction modules. Verified repositories,
owners, commands, dependencies, environments, and operational facts belong in
project context. Client-native paths, metadata, and capability translations
belong in the relevant adapter or integration documentation.

Every documented fact should identify its evidence, confidence, freshness, and
authority policy. Missing or stale evidence remains unknown until verified;
never fill a worksheet with plausible guesses. Keep examples inert and clearly
label templates, worksheets, source material, and active guidance.

When ownership or precedence is ambiguous, record the ambiguity and escalate
instead of silently overriding a nearer or more authoritative instruction.
